The problem

Resolve a differential input with a bounded error budget.

The design targets a 1 Vpp differential input at a 100 MS/s conversion rate. The important challenge was not merely connecting a CDAC, comparator, and controller: each block had to settle and make its decision inside a limited timing budget without consuming the mismatch and noise margin of a 5-bit converter.

The resulting architecture uses a differential CDAC, transmission-gate top-plate sampling, a StrongArm dynamic comparator, reference switching, and SAR control.

Static verification

Linearity was well within half an LSB.

An endpoint-corrected static sweep found no missing codes. The maximum reported INL and DNL were both approximately 0.02492 LSB. The final residue sanity check was approximately 0.0093 LSB.

Dynamic verification

The missing fraction of a bit was the useful result.

At the nominal coherent input frequency (12.109375 MHz), the transistor-level simulation reached 4.52 ENOB. The FFT showed the third harmonic as the dominant non-fundamental component. That points to signal-dependent nonlinearity - rather than white noise alone - as the primary explanation for the remaining gap to an ideal 5-bit result.

Nominal input

The report identifies an aliased HD3 spur as dominant at high input frequency. Sampling-network settling, voltage-dependent switch resistance, clock feedthrough, and comparator kickback are the leading mechanisms to investigate.

What I learned

Good blocks are not automatically a good converter.

Size with interactions in mind

Larger sampling devices improve on-resistance, but increase parasitics and charge injection. The final design balanced these effects against a ~2.85 ns acquisition interval.

Verify beyond nominal

The 200-run comparator Monte Carlo regeneration result and ~0.293% (1σ) unit-capacitor mismatch were useful margins - but did not replace system-level dynamic tests.

Move toward implementation

Rev. B should include transistor-level SAR digital power, layout, DRC/LVS, parasitic extraction, and post-layout PVT/mismatch characterization.
