SAR ADC.

A fully differential top-plate-sampling successive-approximation ADC designed and simulated in 65 nm CMOS.

Scope: Academic, schematic-level transistor simulation. No layout, tapeout, or silicon measurement is claimed.

Design goal

Resolve a differential input with a bounded error budget.

The design targets a 1 Vpp differential input at 100 MS/s. The CDAC, sampling network, comparator, reference switching, and SAR timing had to work as an integrated conversion loop.

Static verification

Linearity was well within half an LSB.

An endpoint-corrected static sweep found no missing codes. Maximum reported INL and DNL were both approximately 0.02492 LSB.

Dynamic verification

The missing fraction of a bit was the useful result.

At 12.109375 MHz input, the FFT reached 4.52 ENOB. The third harmonic was the dominant non-fundamental component, pointing to signal-dependent nonlinearity rather than white noise alone as the leading cause of the gap to ideal 5-bit performance.

Nominal: SNDR 28.98 dB, SFDR 33.91 dB, ENOB 4.52 bits.

Near Nyquist (48.93 MHz): SNDR 26.71 dB, SFDR 28.88 dB, ENOB 4.14 bits.
