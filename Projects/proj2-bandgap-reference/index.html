Evidence

Temperature, supply, startup, and noise.

Temperature sweep: calculated TC of approximately 30.8 ppm/°C.

Supply rejection: 34.98 dB at 10 Hz.

Engineering lesson

The noise miss made the trade-off visible.

The 0.1-10 Hz output-noise result was approximately 122.6 µVpp, above the 50 µVpp target. The reported noise-contribution summary identified PMOS current-source devices as leading flicker-noise contributors. Enlarging them can reduce flicker noise, but adds parasitic capacitance and can slow startup: noise ↔ startup time ↔ power.

Next revision

What I would change before layout.

Rebalance flicker noise

Resize and re-bias the dominant current-source devices, then assess the startup penalty.

Strengthen DC supply behavior

Investigate supply-sensitive bias paths and headroom to reduce line regulation.

Finish physical design

Perform layout, DRC/LVS, extraction, and post-layout PVT/Monte Carlo verification.
